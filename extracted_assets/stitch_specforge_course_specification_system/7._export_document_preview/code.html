Course Overview & Pre-requisites

This core course covers fundamental abstractions, algorithm design strategies, and concrete implementations of linear and non-linear data structures. Students master asymptotic complexity analysis, graph traversals, dynamic programming, and balanced search trees to devise computationally efficient engineering solutions.

Pre-requisites: CS101: Computer Programming in C MA201: Discrete Mathematics

Unit-wise Content & Contact Hours

45 Hours Total
Unit I: Foundations & Linear Structures 9 Hours

Asymptotic notations, recurrences, master theorem; singly/doubly linked lists, circular buffers, stack applications (parsing, evaluation).

Unit II: Trees & Hierarchical Models 10 Hours

Binary search trees, rotations, balanced trees (AVL and Red-Black properties), multi-way trees (B-Trees and B+ Trees) for storage indices.

Unit III: Priority Queues & Hashing 8 Hours

Binary heaps, binomial heaps, Fibonacci heaps, universal hashing schemes, open addressing, collision handling, cuckoo hashing.

Unit IV: Graphs & Network Algorithms 10 Hours

Representations, BFS, DFS, articulation points, topological sort, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall, Kruskal, Prim algorithms.

Unit V: Advanced Design & Intractability 8 Hours

Dynamic programming state equations, greedy choice properties, amortized accounting, intro to NP-completeness and polynomial reductions.
